Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2025

Your privacy matters to us. Speako does not sell your personal data to third parties. Voice recordings are processed in real-time and are not stored permanently on our servers.

1. Who We Are

Speako ("we," "our," or "us") is an AI-powered public speaking coach available on iOS. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Speako app.

If you have any questions, contact us at nobaahass@gmail.com.

2. Permissions We Request

Speako requests the following device permissions:

  • Microphone — required to record your speaking drills. Audio is transmitted to our speech-to-text provider (Groq) for real-time transcription and is not stored permanently.

You may revoke microphone access at any time via Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → Speako. Revoking access will prevent you from completing drills.

3. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you sign in with Apple, we receive your name and email address (or the private relay email Apple generates on your behalf). We use this to create and manage your account.

Voice Recordings

Speako asks you to record short audio responses (typically 25 seconds) during speaking drills. These recordings are transmitted to Groq for real-time speech-to-text transcription. Voice recordings are not stored permanently — they are processed and discarded after transcription.

Usage & Progress Data

We collect data about how you use the app, including:

  • Drills completed and session scores
  • XP points and skill progression levels
  • Practice streak and frequency data
  • App settings and preferences

Subscription & Purchase Data

Subscription management is handled by RevenueCat and Apple. We receive confirmation of your subscription status (active, expired, trial) but do not store your payment card details — those are managed entirely by Apple.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and personalise the Speako coaching experience
  • To generate AI feedback on your speaking drills
  • To track your progress and unlock levels
  • To manage your account and subscription
  • To send important service updates (not marketing, unless you opt in)
  • To improve the app based on usage patterns

5. Third-Party Services

Speako uses the following third-party services, each subject to their own privacy policies:

  • Groq — voice transcription (speech-to-text). Audio is sent to Groq for processing.
  • Anthropic Claude — AI coaching responses. Transcribed text is sent to Claude to generate feedback.
  • ElevenLabs — text-to-speech. AI feedback text is sent to ElevenLabs to generate the coach's voice.
  • Supabase — backend infrastructure, authentication, and data storage.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management and purchase tracking.
  • Apple Sign In — authentication.

We do not share your personal information with any third parties beyond what is necessary to operate these services.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account and progress data for as long as your account is active. Voice recordings are not retained after transcription. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

7. Children's Privacy

Speako is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with their data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data. All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your data is restricted to authorised personnel only.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including:

  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you
  • The right to correct inaccurate data
  • The right to request deletion of your data
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email us at nobaahass@gmail.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or by email. Continued use of Speako after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
